Cavernum

Pair numbers. Clear the cave. Dig deeper.

Cavernum is a free number matching puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Match tiles that are equal or add up to ten and connect them by a clear path, clear rows as you go, and work your way through increasingly difficult cave puzzles.

How to play Cavernum

Simple rule. Hard puzzle.

Tap two tiles that are equal in value or add up to ten. The two numbers must also be connected by a clear path. Clear enough of the board and rows compress, revealing new pairings underneath.

Built to get harder.

Cavernum is built for players who find many casual number match games too easy to stay interesting. New blockers and irregular board shapes are introduced gradually, then combined so later levels demand planning as well as number recognition.

Equal or sum to ten

Match identical values or two numbers whose values total ten.

Find a clear path

Knowing the pair is only half the puzzle. The route between the tiles has to work too.

Outsmart blockers

Voids, rocks, gates and changing board shapes force you to rethink obvious matches.

Use utilities when stuck

Hint, Undo, Swap and Add Numbers give you options when a board tightens up.

Why Cavernum

A number game with route-finding.

Cavernum combines number-pair recognition with spatial reasoning. A pair can be mathematically valid and still be unusable because the path is blocked. That makes each board a route-finding problem as well as a matching puzzle.

Every one of the 60 launch levels was solved by hand before release. That verifies that each shipped level has a solution without implying that every path a player chooses will succeed.

Cavernum FAQ

How do you match numbers in Cavernum?

Tap two tiles that are equal in value or add up to ten. The pair must also be connected by a clear path.

Is Cavernum free?

Yes. Cavernum is free to download and play. It contains optional advertising and in-app purchases.

Is Cavernum available on Android?

Yes. Cavernum is live on Google Play for Android.

Is Cavernum available on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Cavernum is live on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Do matching numbers have to be adjacent?

No. The important requirement is that the two matching tiles can be connected by a valid clear path.
